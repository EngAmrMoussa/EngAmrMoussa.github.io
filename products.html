AM Infra Batch Tool v1.0.0

A Civil 3D batch automation add-in — replaces repetitive, one-at-a-time workflows with real batch operations.

What it does

Replaces repetitive, one-at-a-time Civil 3D workflows with real batch operations — pick your inputs once, apply them across everything at once, and get back to the actual engineering.

  • Batch AlignmentsCreate alignments from multiple selected entities in one pass.
  • Batch Profile ViewsBuild profile views with sampled surfaces across many alignments at once.
Compatibility: Civil 3D 2025 through 2027 Who it's for: Civil/Infrastructure engineers and designers running gravity or pressure pipe networks

See it in action

Rainbow Plan — color-coded utility offsets from an alignment
